This Cookie Policy explains how **MSP Hub Technologies, LLC** uses cookies and similar technologies on msphub.com.

## How this site uses cookies

- **Strictly necessary.** The site works without setting tracking cookies. Any cookie needed for a page to function (for example, to remember a consent choice) is set only for that purpose.
- **Analytics.** Where site analytics are enabled, analytics cookies are set only after consent in regions that require it. Visitors who decline are not tracked.

## Controlling cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how parts of the site work.
