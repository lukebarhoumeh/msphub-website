This Privacy Policy covers personal information handled by **MSP Hub Technologies, LLC** (formerly READY Networks) through the msphub.com website, the HubX platform, and the services we deliver.

## What this site collects

- **Forms.** When you request a demo, a quote, a service inquiry, a partner application, or a comparison of your Microsoft invoice, we collect what you type into the form and the page it was sent from, and we use it to reply to you. Submissions are delivered to our sales team.
- **Uploaded invoices.** Invoices and license exports you upload for a comparison are stored privately and shared with our deal desk through an expiring link. They are never published.
- **Analytics.** If site analytics are enabled, they run only with the consent required in your region.

## Questions

Privacy questions reach us at [sales@msphub.com](mailto:sales@msphub.com).
