In April 2025 the team, then still ReadyNetworks, took its licensing platform to ChannelPro LIVE in Chicago and came home with an <NeedsConfirm id="awardTitle" fallback="Editor's Choice award">Editor's Choice award (exact title and category to be confirmed)</NeedsConfirm>.

Thank you to every MSP and VAR who stopped by and told us what they wanted a licensing platform to do next.

## What MSP Hub brings to MSPs today

- A licensing platform **built by an MSP for MSPs**: every customer tenant, subscription, renewal, and invoice in one place.
- **Tier-1 direct pricing** from a Microsoft CSP direct-bill partner, with partner price and MSRP side by side for every SKU.
- **Customer-facing invoices** generated for the partner, with the partner's own remittance details, and margin per customer on every statement.
- **A services bench behind the partner**: migrations, Azure, security, and managed operations delivered under the partner's relationship.

## Since then

In July 2025 ReadyNetworks became MSP Hub, and in October 2026 the platform was redesigned and renamed **HubX, powered by MSP Hub**. The [HubX page](/hubx) covers what it does today, and the [partner programs](/partners) explain how MSPs, agents, and direct-bill CSPs work with us.
