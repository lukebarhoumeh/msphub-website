READY is now known as MSP Hub.

In 1999 we started as ReadyNetworks, a managed services provider. Along the way we built our own licensing platform, because the tools the distributors gave us were not good enough for the way we wanted to run Microsoft licensing for our customers. The platform became a marketplace built by an MSP for MSPs, and the company grew into a Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider (CSP) direct-bill partner that bills, supports, and provisions its customers itself.

In July 2025 the company became **MSP Hub**. One name now covers the licensing, the services, and the platform.

## What changes

- **The name.** ReadyNetworks is now MSP Hub. You will see "MSP Hub Technologies, LLC" on commercial paperwork and "formerly READY Networks" alongside it while the new name settles in.
- **One front door.** Microsoft licensing, professional services, managed services, and development now sit under one brand and one team.

## What does not change

- **The people.** The same engineers, licensing desk, and account teams.
- **The Microsoft relationship.** MSP Hub remains a Microsoft CSP direct-bill partner (Tier-1 Direct) in North America: no distributor layer in the price or in the escalation path.
- **Your agreements.** Existing subscriptions, terms, and renewal dates carry on exactly as they are.

## Update, November 2026

The platform has moved on as well. In October 2026 the MSP Hub platform was redesigned and renamed **HubX, powered by MSP Hub**. Existing accounts and data carried over. Read about it on the [HubX page](/hubx), or [log in to HubX](https://hubx.msphub.com).
